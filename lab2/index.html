Design Justification

I designed a lollipop-style chart to avoid a standard scatter plot while still making quantitative comparisons clear. I mapped population (ratio) to horizontal position and line length because length and position on a common axis are easy to compare across cities. I mapped region (nominal) to color so viewers can quickly group cities by category. I mapped development level (ordinal) to stroke thickness and opacity, allowing Low, Medium, and High to appear with increasing visual emphasis. I mapped temperature (interval) to circle size at the end of each line, so warmer and cooler cities are easy to distinguish without adding another axis. I also included tooltips with city names and exact values, so viewers can identify each city and inspect details while still seeing broader patterns in one chart.
